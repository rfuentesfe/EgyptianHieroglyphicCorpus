# Corpus authorship, provenance and data notice

The Gardiner-code/English corpus was manually constructed, reviewed, normalized and adapted for this research using scholarly resources as reference material, together with manually prepared notes and work on hieroglyphic stelae.

The published Gardiner-code/English pairs were manually curated and adapted for this research rather than automatically copied from those resources. The reproducible preparation script subsequently normalizes whitespace, removes incomplete entries, deduplicates exact pairs and creates the controlled splits from the manually prepared source corpus.

## Reference materials

- Schweitzer, S. D. (2019). *AED – Ancient Egyptian Dictionary Version 1.0*. Zenodo.
- Erman, A., & Grapow, H. (1926–1963). *Wörterbuch der ägyptischen Sprache*.
- Gardiner's *Egyptian Grammar* and dictionary resources.
- The authors' own notes and manual work on hieroglyphic stelae.

This statement describes provenance at corpus level. The released CSVs contain Gardiner-code/English pairs and do not provide an item-level mapping to the reference materials.
